EMAIL AND INVESTIGATION TIMELINE
================================

Case:
Scenario 06 - Phishing Email Investigation

Time standard:
UTC is used where an exact time and timezone are available.
Dates without an exact time are retained as date-only observations.

Important limitation:
The sample contains conflicting date indicators. The email Date header
must not be treated as independently verified delivery time because it is
sender-controlled. Both candidate periods must therefore be considered
when searching organisational telemetry.

2. EMAIL DATE INDICATORS
------------------------

2022-12-09 08:58:26 UTC

Event:
The email Date header contained:

Thu, 9 Dec 2022 09:58:26 +0100

Interpretation:
This converts to 08:58:26 UTC. However, the Date header is generated by
the sender or sending system and is not sufficient by itself to establish
the actual organisational receipt time.

If this date accurately represents the email event, the message appeared
during the period in which URLhaus was still recording the payload URL as
online.


2022-12-12

Event:
URLhaus recorded this as the last date on which the malicious URL was
reported online.

Interpretation:
This is a historical threat-intelligence observation. It does not prove
that the URL was continuously reachable before this date or permanently
unreachable after it.


2023-11-27

Event:
An alternative candidate date was identified during analysis of the
sample artifacts.

Source:
See timestamp-consistency.txt

Interpretation:
This conflicts with the 2022 Date header. If November 2023 represents the
actual processing period, the URL had already been reported offline by
URLhaus for several months.

The available EML alone does not resolve which candidate date represents
the actual delivery or processing time.

-----------------------------

Result:

Interpretation:
This confirms historical malicious reputation. It does not establish the

2026-07-27 NZST

Event:
The investigator completed passive review of the email sample, URLhaus,

Actions:

- Reviewed historical threat-intelligence records.

4. EVIDENCE-BASED EVENT SEQUENCE
--------------------------------

1. The malicious URL existed and was recorded by public threat-intelligence
   services from October 2022.

2. The investigated email contained the same complete URL and used a
   financial purchase-related social-engineering pretext.

3. The exact organisational delivery time cannot be established because
   the available sample contains conflicting date indicators and no
   authoritative email-gateway records were provided.

4. No evidence establishes that a recipient clicked the link, downloaded
   the executable, or executed a payload.

5. Passive threat intelligence confirms that the message was malicious,
   but organisational impact remains unknown.


5. TELEMETRY SEARCH WINDOWS
---------------------------

Primary candidate window:
2022-12-09 through 2022-12-12 UTC

Alternative candidate window:
Around 2023-11-27 UTC

Recommended approach:
Search both periods in email-gateway, mailbox, DNS, proxy, firewall, and
endpoint records. Include a reasonable time buffer before and after each
candidate date to account for timezone conversion, delayed processing,
and clock inconsistencies.


6. TIMELINE LIMITATIONS
-----------------------

The timeline combines sender-controlled email metadata, sample artifacts,
historical public intelligence, and analyst activity. These evidence types
have different levels of reliability.

Without original email-gateway trace data, mailbox audit records, or
endpoint telemetry, the timeline cannot establish:

- Authoritative receipt or delivery time.
- Intended or actual organisational recipients.
- User interaction.
- Payload download or execution.
- Endpoint or account compromise.
