IMPACT ASSESSMENT
=================

Case:
Scenario 06 - Phishing Email Investigation

Assessment scope:
This assessment is based on the original EML sample, email-header and
body analysis, extracted indicators, and passive threat intelligence.

No email-gateway, DNS, proxy, firewall, endpoint, identity, or mailbox
audit logs were available.

1. CONFIRMED FINDINGS
---------------------

- The email used financial and purchase-related social-engineering content.
- The message contained a link directly referencing a Windows executable:
  hxxp://107[.]175[.]247[.]199/loader/install[.]exe
- The complete URL has established historical malicious reputation.
- The hosting IP has supporting historical malicious reputation.
- The message represents a malicious phishing-based malware-delivery attempt.

3. ASSESSMENT
-------------

The available evidence establishes a malicious phishing-based
malware-delivery attempt. It does not establish successful delivery to a
specific mailbox, user interaction, payload execution, or compromise.

The email verdict is high-confidence malicious, but maliciousness and
impact must not be treated as the same conclusion. Actual organisational
impact remains unknown because the telemetry required to confirm or
exclude post-delivery activity was not available.

This case must therefore be classified as a confirmed malicious email
with unconfirmed impact, rather than as a confirmed endpoint or account
compromise.

4. DATA REQUIRED TO DETERMINE IMPACT
------------------------------------

Email gateway and mailbox records:
- Search the Message-ID, sender, subject, malicious URL, purchase reference,
  and access code.
- Identify intended recipients, delivery status, quarantine actions, and
  other matching messages.

DNS, proxy, and firewall logs:
- Search for requests or connections involving 107[.]175[.]247[.]199.
- Review both candidate time periods identified in timestamp-consistency.txt.

Endpoint telemetry:
- Search for creation or execution of install.exe.
- Review browser downloads, browser child processes, file hashes,
  persistence mechanisms, and subsequent outbound connections.

Identity and mailbox audit logs:
- Review abnormal sign-ins, session activity, mailbox rules, and forwarding
  changes if additional evidence suggests account involvement.

5. ESCALATION CONDITION
-----------------------

If URL access, payload download, or execution is identified, escalate the
case to a potential endpoint compromise investigation and isolate the
affected host where appropriate.

If no matching activity is found in sufficiently complete historical logs,
record the result as no evidence of successful compromise, not proof that
no compromise occurred.

6. FINAL IMPACT STATEMENT
-------------------------

Impact:
Unknown / not established

Compromise status:
Not confirmed

Reason:
The malicious delivery mechanism is confirmed, but no telemetry was
available to establish recipient interaction or post-delivery activity.
