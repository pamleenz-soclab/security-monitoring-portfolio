# Rule Selection Record

Ten candidates were reviewed. Six were selected to balance authentication, endpoint, web/network, cloud privilege, tuning, correlation and deduplication.

Selected: R19-01 (Scenario 17), R19-02 (Scenario 12), R19-03 (Scenario 15), R19-04 (Scenario 18), R19-05 (Scenario 11), and R19-06 (Scenario 16).

Repeated SSH failures were reserved because Scenario 17 provided richer structured authentication evidence. Scenario 10 PowerShell was reserved because Scenario 12 already had stronger rule/evidence lifecycle material. Additional Scenario 18 cloud detections were reserved to avoid turning Scenario 19 into a cloud-only portfolio and to preserve Scenario 20 boundaries.
