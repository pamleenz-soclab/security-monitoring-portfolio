## Query-design principle

Hunting logic may intentionally be broader than alert logic. HC-07 is the clearest example: the hunt searched for SQLi-family transactions outside R19-03 high-confidence branches without weakening R19-03 itself.

Native parsing, scheduling, suppression, ingestion-time field mappings, and production query cost were not executed or measured in this scenario.
